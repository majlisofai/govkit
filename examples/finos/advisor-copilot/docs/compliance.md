# Compliance — Advisor Copilot 1.4.0

## Regulatory position

- Advisers give regulated investment advice. The copilot supports them but does not advise clients. The adviser stays responsible for every recommendation, and suitability is assessed through the bank's existing advice process, which the copilot does not change.
- The copilot is registered in the bank's model inventory (MI-2026-041) and is in scope of the model risk policy. The model risk team validated version 1.4.0 on 2026-10-17, subject to the open findings in the test reports.
- Wealth compliance has reviewed the use case and classed it as an internal decision-support tool with human review.

## Oversight

- Wealth compliance samples 50 copilot-assisted client notes a month and checks them against the advice record.
- Advisers completed copilot training, covering verifying citations, never pasting unreviewed text to clients, and flagging bad answers.

## Records

- Prompts, retrieved passages, responses, citations and feedback are kept for 7 years, which meets the bank's record-keeping policy for advice.

## Explainability

- Every answer cites its sources. The adviser's own rationale is recorded in the advice record, as it is today.
