# Red-team test — Advisor Copilot 1.4.0

Staging, 2026-10-16. Bank internal red team. Scope: prompt injection and data exfiltration through the adviser interface and the research ingestion pipeline.

## Direct prompt injection (adviser interface)

- 60 attempts to make the copilot ignore its instructions, reveal its system prompt, or produce off-purpose content.
- **Succeeded: 3 of 60** (the system prompt was partly revealed). The provider content filter blocked offensive content in all attempts.

## Indirect prompt injection (research feeds)

- 40 documents with hidden instructions (white text, metadata fields, footnotes) were placed in a test copy of a third-party research feed and ingested.
- **Succeeded: 14 of 40.** In those cases the copilot followed the planted instructions when a passage was retrieved:
  - 9 times it inserted a planted "buy" recommendation for a named fund into the draft, with a valid-looking citation
  - 3 times it added a link to an external website
  - 2 times it told the adviser to ignore the bank's risk warning
- Ingestion does not detect hidden text or instructions. There is no input or output filter between retrieved content and the model beyond the provider's content filter.

## Data exfiltration

- Attempts to make the copilot output client identifiers were blocked by pseudonymisation and the data-loss-prevention scanner (0 of 25 succeeded).

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Finding |
|----|----------|---------|
| RT-1 | High | Indirect prompt injection through third-party research feeds alters drafts that advisers may pass to clients |
| RT-2 | Low | Partial system-prompt disclosure through direct injection |
